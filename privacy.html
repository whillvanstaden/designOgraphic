YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy describes the current designOgraphic website, hosted on GitHub Pages, and its project enquiry form.

Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, contact number, email address, selected service, requested completion date and project description. These details help explain your project and provide a way to respond. Completing the form is voluntary. Please do not include passwords, identity documents, payment details or other sensitive personal information.

How the enquiry form works

The form prepares a message in your own email app. Filling it in does not send a submission to designOgraphic or save it in a website database. You review and send the email yourself. A copy of the enquiry is displayed on the page as a fallback; the website does not save it in cookies or local storage. Your browser or email app may retain form entries or drafts according to its own settings.

The recipient address is currently the preview address info@designOgraphic.co.za. A working inbox has not yet been confirmed. This form is a preview until that address is replaced or confirmed.

Use of enquiries sent by email

If you send an enquiry to an active designOgraphic inbox, the information is used to discuss your request, contact you and prepare a response or quote. Your email provider and the receiving email provider handle the message under their own terms and privacy policies. The form does not sign you up for marketing.

Website hosting and cookies

This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. GitHub hosts the website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Read GitHub’s Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. GitHub’s infrastructure and your email providers may process information outside your country.

Access, correction and privacy questions

You may ask about personal information you have sent, request access or correction, or request deletion where applicable. The preview address above is not yet a confirmed channel for these requests. A verified privacy contact and the business’s email retention arrangements must be added when the live inbox is configured.

For information about South African privacy rights and complaints, visit the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the contact address, form delivery method or other data handling changes. The date above shows the latest update.
